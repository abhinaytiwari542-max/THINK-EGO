Egocentric data to train the next generation of physical AI.

We're a data operation for physical AI. We record egocentric, first-person video of real tasks in real environments, and turn it into training data for robots and embodied AI. Contract-based, shot to spec, priced per hour of usable footage.

The problem

Physical AI is bottlenecked by data, not models.

Robotics is moving faster than ever. But good robots need egocentric, first-person data of real tasks in real environments, and almost nobody can collect it at the scale, cost, and specificity training actually needs. Most available footage is staged, simulated, or shot from the wrong point of view.

How it works

Capture now. Simulate on demand, later.

Today, it's focused capture. A client specifies the task, the environment, and the format, and we go get it. Later, it becomes a standing library: any process recreated and generated on request, without a fresh shoot every time.
